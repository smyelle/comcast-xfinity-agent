Driven 2026-08-13 against app 1675b42e (comcast-ux-local-specialists), voice, cold.

WHAT WAS DRIVEN
  tests/scope_voice_probe.py — one continuous audio buffer: the complaint, the account
  number, N seconds of silence, an optional hesitation ("uh"), then the spoken answer to
  the scoping question. Scored on the engine's own `filled["wifi_scope_early"]`, not on
  the transcript.

THE RESULT, BY SHAPE OF THE CALLER'S TURN

  caller speaks ONCE during the wait          captured 5 of 5
    --answer-at 12 (x3), 15 (x1), 17 with --hesitate "" (x1)

  caller speaks TWICE — a hesitation, then the answer   captured 0 of 8
    --answer-at 17 (x6), 19 (x1), and 17 with --hesitate-gap 3 / 6 (x2)

  The gap between the two utterances does not matter: 0.8s, 3s and 6s all lose it.

WHY IT IS NOT THE REASSURANCE LINE
  The first suspicion was the `while_waiting` ladder speaking over the caller, which is
  what the live call showed ("Uh" at 45.4s, the line at 46.1s, the answer at 46.3s, and
  a barge-in recorded on the platform's VAD span). The framework fix for that landed and
  was driven: the line is gone from these runs and the answer is STILL lost, 0 of 4.
  It is worth having on its own merits — talking at a caller who is answering you is
  wrong however it ends — but it is not the cause.

WHY IT IS NOT THE DRIVER
  CONTROL, same continuous buffer, no wait running: complaint, "uh", 3s, the account
  number. The agent re-asked after "uh" and then took the account number normally —
  "Thanks." and the sweep started. A second utterance in one buffer is fine; a second
  utterance DURING A WAIT is not.

WHERE IT IS LOST
  Not in the engine, and not in the model. Pulled the conversation for a failing run
  (scopevoice-1786625370-1) and read the engine's own inputs per invocation:

    t3 #12  last='fire'  scanned='fire'  n_user_turns=3      <- the hesitation, ASR'd
    t4 #13  last='fire'  scanned='fire'  n_user_turns=3      <- the ANSWER's turn
    t5 #14  last='fire'  scanned='fire'  n_user_turns=3
    t6 #15  last='fire'  scanned='fire'  n_user_turns=3

  The conversation record carries t4 as `user: "Everything seems to be having trouble."`
  The agent was INVOKED for it. `llm_request.contents` never received it —
  `n_user_turns` and `scanned_user_text` are frozen from the hesitation onward, for the
  rest of the call. Nothing downstream of the request can recover a turn that is not in
  it: not the cue matcher, not the model, not a setter.

  Identical in shape to the original live call this was found on
  (simulator-031307e8, app 1675b42e): "Uh" at t4, the real answer at t5, and every later
  invocation reading 'Uh'.

WHAT WAS TRIED AND REJECTED
  Letting the model take the wait turn instead of holding — the one lever the engine has
  left. Built, pushed and driven: the model answered the hesitation with an emoji and
  then "Hmm, I'm having trouble with that", the platform's crash envelope, and the
  answer was lost anyway. Reverted. The silent hold is right; the loss is upstream of it.

WHAT THE CALLER GETS TODAY
