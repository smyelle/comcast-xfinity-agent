`fetch_customer_context` fixture answer, and the fixture is taught cujs.yaml's account ->
scenario bindings at build time.

CONTROL, same day, same driver: the DEFAULT build (590a468f) driven cold still answers
"I see an issue with your account status" on every account, because the context hub errors
in dev. That build is unchanged by design -- it is the one that talks to real backends.

MARGIN. The verdict lands 43.5-51.3s into the call and the account is captured at
~17s, so the caller's silence has to outlast ~26-34s of sweep. 45s and 60s clear it every
time; 30s is a coin flip (2 of 4 attempts showed the verdict, 2 did not, one of those with
the session returning nothing at all afterwards). Do not verify this journey on a single
30s pass.
